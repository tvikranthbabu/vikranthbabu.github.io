How I work

I lead lean science and ML engineering teams and stay close to the technical work, while setting the roadmap, success measures and priorities with senior stakeholders. At eBay that was a team of about seven across London, Seattle and Shanghai, three of them reporting to me directly.

Frame the problem before the model

I start with the decision the work serves and whether it is the right lever. On eBay's seller messaging, that meant naming the business north star up front and keeping the science metric honest about what copy alone can move.

Design the evaluation with the system

The launch bar, the offline evaluation and the online test are part of the design, decided before the build. Evaluation is the part of the work I care most about, from Bayesian A/B testing for hotel-ads bidding to an LLM judge checked against human review.

Own the outcome

I stay accountable once something is live: production, the readout, and the decision that follows, including when the answer is to stop.
